Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD)

Precision drilling for crossings, limited-access areas, and sensitive surfaces.

Benefits

  • Reduced surface disruption versus open cut
  • Ideal for roads, rail, waterways, and landscaped areas
  • Lower restoration footprint and improved site access
  • Better routing options in congested corridors

Common use cases

  • Conduit placement for fiber and telecom
  • Water and gas crossings
  • Electrical conduit installs and upgrades
  • Industrial site runs where downtime matters

Benefits

  • Strong field communication and daily progress alignment
  • Quality checks built into the workflow

Common use cases

  • Conduit, pipe, and casing installs
  • Handholes, vaults, and access points
  • Utility relocations and corridor upgrades
  • New development infrastructure

Trenchless Solutions

When the surface can’t be disrupted, trenchless methods keep stakeholders happy.

Benefits

  • Reduced restoration and less impact to traffic flow
  • Workable in tight corridors and near existing assets
  • Strong option for sensitive surfaces and landscaping
  • Improved schedule predictability with less excavation

Common use cases

  • Crossings under roads and driveways
  • Corridors with heavy congestion
  • Sites with limited staging and access
  • Projects requiring minimal disruption
